Troubleshooting

DANGER
Smoke, burning smell or sparks: press E-STOP, then switch the supply output off.
Smoke, burning smell, spark or unusual sound
Press the E-STOP immediately. Then supply output off, generator output off. Report to the setup owner.
No output current, supply draw near idle
E-STOP is pressed (OFF): rotate it clockwise to release (ON). Check supply output is on and generator output is on.
Supply in CC, voltage dropping
3.5 A limit reached. Amplitude down to 700 mVpp, supply output off, check the loop for a short. If the loop is fine, report.
Supply shows a protection message
Supply output off and report to the setup owner. Do not change the protection settings.
Generator settings look wrong
Utility → Recall → Setup 0, then compare the screen with the settings table.
Current is half or double the expected value
Generator load must be High Z. Recall Setup 0.
Scope shows only half a cycle
Normal after Auto Scale. Turn the Horizontal knob until full cycles are visible.
Looks like two sines overlapping
Switching noise. Press Run/Stop to freeze the trace.
Pk-Pk, Max or Min readings jump around
They include switching noise. Freeze the trace and use the Y cursors instead.
Offset outside ±5 mV at 10 kHz, 700 mVpp
Shut down and report to the setup owner.
Waveform changes when someone is near the board
Hands off the PCB. If it persists, amplitude to 700 mVpp, shut down and report.
Amplitude jumps without input change
Reduce amplitude, shut down, report.
Lower current at high frequency
Expected: |Z| rises with frequency. Use the calculator for the new amplitude.
Hotspot MAX reaches 85 °C
Amplitude back to 700 mVpp and let the fans cool it. Keep fan inlets clear.
